Your Safe Space to Talk and Heal

TalkToCare is a pan-India initiative dedicated to bridging the gap between students and mental health professionals. We believe every student deserves access to quality mental healthcare to navigate academic pressure, personal challenges, and the journey of self-discovery.

1. Find Your Therapist

Browse profiles of our verified and compassionate therapists from all over India. Find the right fit for your needs.

2. Book a Session

Choose a convenient time and date. Our flexible scheduling works around your academic calendar.

3. Connect via Video Call

Join your secure and private video session from anywhere. All you need is a stable internet connection.
